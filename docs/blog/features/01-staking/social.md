## LinkedIn

Most crypto in a wallet just sits there. Today we gave it a job: **staking is now live on FairWins.**

Open Finance → Earn → Staking, pick an asset, and put it to work — without ever handing us custody.

Two ways to stake:

- **Liquid** — stake ETH with Lido (receive wstETH) or POL with Polygon's sPOL (receive sPOL). Your liquid token grows in value and stays movable.
- **Delegated** — delegate POL to a hand-picked, vetted Polygon validator and earn its rewards directly.

We built it around one principle we don't bend on — never imply something the chain hasn't done:

- Estimated APR and any platform fee are shown **before you sign** — and you're never charged more than the rate you saw.
- The fee is **liquid-only and transparent**; delegated staking is fee-free, and a zero rate shows no fee line at all.
- **You can always exit.** Unstake, withdraw, and claim keep working even during an emergency pause — because those paths never route through our contracts.
- Unbonding periods and slashing risk are stated plainly, up front.
- It's **non-custodial** end to end: a stake completes atomically or reverts, and your funds never rest in our hands between transactions.

Behind the scenes, an on-chain control surface lets us pause new staking instantly in an incident (exits untouched), with a multisig-held, fully-audited trail of every operator action.
